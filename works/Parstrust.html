Pars Trust

Mobile Crypto Trading App

Role:

Lead UI/UX Designer, User Research, Wireframing, Prototyping, Design System, Usability Testing

Tools:

Figma, FigJam

Timeline:

2022

Project overview

As the Lead UI/UX Designer, my objective was to architect a user-friendly, high-trust cryptocurrency trading app that simplifies digital asset management, real-time market tracking, and trade execution. The design focuses on progressive disclosure, balancing advanced trading capabilities with a frictionless experience for both novice and experienced investors.

Problem

Reducing Financial Anxiety and Cognitive Overload

Cryptocurrency platforms notoriously overwhelm users with high-density data, fragmented execution flows, and confusing financial terminology. For beginners, this intimidation factor leads to severe onboarding drop-offs and trading errors, while experienced traders suffer from sluggish, non-intuitive interfaces.

Solutions

Demystifying Digital Finance

  • Progressive Disclosure: Pars Trust surfaces core actions and simplified balances first, hiding complex data layers until requested by the user.
  • Strict Semantic Guardrails: Implemented a rigorous visual hierarchy where green and red indicators are strictly reserved for live price movements, completely isolating them from button states or general UI actions to prevent user confusion.

User persona & Insights

Pain points:

  • Finds standard crypto trading apps overly complex, intimidating, and prone to accidental high-risk taps.
  • Struggles to track overall portfolio performance across volatile multi-asset holdings in real-time.
  • Distrusts platforms that lack transparent feedback or clear compliance indicators.

Motivations:

  • Wants to grow wealth through crypto securely without spending weeks deciphering complex trading terminals.
  • Needs a reliable, high-trust platform with clear compliance and instant verification workflows.
  • Prefers a clean, distraction-free interface built on a scalable, robust design system.

Visual Design & Key Features

The visual language was intentionally kept minimal and modern, leveraging clear cards, generous whitespace, and high-contrast typography to inspire absolute user confidence during financial transactions.

Onboarding

Frictionless Entry to Crypto

Designed a fast, engaging onboarding sequence introducing core value propositions and guiding users seamlessly into profile creation without unnecessary cognitive fatigue.

Authorization & Compliance (KYC)

Engineered a secure, multi-layered verification pipeline balancing regulatory compliance with high conversion rates:

  • Fast Authentication: Social logins (Google, Apple) and secure OTP email verification.
  • Streamlined KYC: Real-time camera document scanning (ID/Passport) with instant framing guidance.
  • Enhanced Security: Custom PIN setup and biometric authentication (Fingerprint/FaceID) readiness.

Home / Dashboard

A user-centric hub displaying overall asset distribution, quick actions, and real-time portfolio summaries engineered to reduce daily trading friction.

Market & Discovery

Advanced filtering and search mechanisms allowing users to sort digital assets instantly by performance, market cap, and custom watchlists.

Streamlined Trading Flow

Simplified buying and selling execution steps, turning complex multi-step crypto conversions into clear, transparent actions.

Outcomes & Impact

  • Simplified Onboarding: Reduced user drop-off during the KYC and account creation phases through real-time optical scanning guidance.
  • Optimized Error Prevention: Prevented accidental double-submission of transactions via strict button-state locking mechanics.
  • Scalable Component Architecture: Established a reusable mobile design system that accelerated developer handoff and ensured multi-screen visual harmony.
